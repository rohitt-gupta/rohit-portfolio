---
title: "Rebuilding my portfolio in five days"
publishedAt: "2026-09-23"
summary: "My old portfolio was almost three years old and built from a YouTube video. This is how I finally rebuilt it."
---

My old portfolio was almost three years old. I built it by watching
[a YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sUKptmUVIBM) end to end while I was learning
Next.js. That was the story. I just built it.

I had very little experience in web development back then. I was a very new web developer. I started
from React and was still learning Next.js, which wasn't used in my full-time job, so it was a new
thing for me, and I learned it from that video.

After building that portfolio, I grew so much as an engineer, as a developer and as a person. I
worked with a lot of influential people. I worked for a lot of agencies, with people worldwide,
especially from America. I worked for one of the frontier AI labs. I did a bunch of amazing stuff at
[Alpine](https://www.alpine.inc). I grew a lot on [Twitter](https://x.com/whyrohitwhy), grew my
following, made a community and a lot of new friends. I moved to Vienna. I learned how editing works
and how [ProseMirror](https://prosemirror.net) works. I fell in love with programming, with
performance optimization, with how engineering works. I started reading blogs.

One thing remained the same through all of it: I was not able to change my portfolio.

There were two things that were nearly impossible for me. The first was working on something that
wasn't paying me. Any side project, any side hustle. My hourly rate was so high that I used to count
my life in hours and in the number of dollars I could earn, rather than spending it on watching a
movie or something like that.

The second was this website. In between all of that, I opened my own design, development and
marketing agency, [Launchbox Studio](https://www.launchbox.studio/?ref=rohitt.in). I hired a lot of
people. I worked with designers, graphic designers and video editors, worked with a lot of clients,
and pitched to a lot of Indian and US based clients. But I still wasn't updating my portfolio.

It was something I always wanted to do. I also wanted to write blogs, but I always wanted to post
them on my own website, and my website was very old. It was cheap and not at all worth it. Still, a
lot of people kept coming. It's deployed on Vercel, so I know how many people come, and honestly I
was shocked.

I also wasn't able to find the perfect inspiration. How many elements do I want? What kind of UI do I
want?

But now, with AI, that has completely changed. Now I really know what my taste is. I know how much I
love minimalism, how much I love playing around with things on UI, and how much I love product
engineering. Somewhere in all of this I started as a front-end engineer, became a back-end
developer, and now I'm a product engineer, which is a completely different thing.

So this time I took my chance. I started building this portfolio five days ago, on the 18th of
September. I started collecting everything I needed: all the inspiration, all the portfolios, all the
data. They're on the [inspiration page](/inspiration). As a developer I've grown enough to know that
planning is more important than starting to execute, so I started with a pen and paper and wrote down
every single thing I needed on my website.

I decided I'd just launch whatever I built by the end of the weekend. I kept sitting down, building,
and posting about it on Twitter, and it turned out I made a lot of progress. It was 90% done on
Monday. Today is Wednesday, the 23rd of September, and I just finished the whole portfolio.

With a day job, it becomes even harder. And unlike a lot of people, I enjoy my work. I really enjoy
doing engineering and I really enjoy working with my team. So the last 10% took a bit longer, and
that 10% completes with the last word of this blog.

I really enjoyed the process of redesigning the whole website. I was not sure what I wanted, but in
the end it turned out to be one of the most interesting portfolios, I guess.

I set aside all the work I was doing on [Flowy](https://flowyhealth.com/?ref=rohitt.in). I still
have a lot of things to build and improve there, but I kept everything aside and wanted to build this
first, so that people can really know me, and so I can keep posting blogs. I really need to write
something, man.

So yeah, this is how I rebuilt my portfolio in five days.
